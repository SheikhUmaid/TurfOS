Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? We'll answer it on a call.

Talk to us
Do customers need to download an application?

No. Your booking app is a progressive web app (PWA): customers open your booking link in their phone's browser and book straight away. They can add it to their home screen if they like, but there's nothing to install from an app store.

Can I manage online and walk-in bookings together?

Yes. Walk-in and phone bookings are added from your dashboard and go into the same calendar as online bookings. A booked slot is blocked for everyone, so the same slot can't be sold twice.

Can I manage multiple pitches?

Yes. Basic covers 1 pitch, Growth up to 3 and Business up to 6. Each pitch has its own slots, pricing and availability, and you can offer multiple sports.

Can customers pay online?

Yes. Online payment is built into the booking flow on every plan, and each booking's payment status is updated in your dashboard. Partial (advance) payments can be enabled where you need them.

Can I change slot timings and pricing?

Yes. You manage time slots and prices from your dashboard, and you can price sessions differently — for example, mornings, afternoons and floodlit evenings.

What happens when my subscription expires?

New bookings and changes are paused until you renew. Your existing data stays in your account and can still be viewed, and everything resumes as soon as the subscription is renewed.

Can I upgrade my subscription?

Yes. You can request a plan change from the subscription page in your dashboard or by contacting us. Your bookings, customers and settings carry over.

Do I get a separate booking website?

Yes. Every facility gets its own booking URL to share with customers on WhatsApp, Instagram or Google. A custom domain can be set up too; any domain costs are separate.

Are payment gateway charges included?

No. Transaction charges from the payment gateway are separate from the TurfOS subscription. A one-time activation cost of ₹4,000 also applies for the first month. OTP/SMS usage and custom domain costs may also be charged separately, as set out in your final commercial terms.
